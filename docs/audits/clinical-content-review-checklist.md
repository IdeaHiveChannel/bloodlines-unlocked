## Review method

- Homepage emergency wording and the instruction to call 112 or attend the nearest emergency department.
- Stroke, brain aneurysm, brain AVM/AVF, DVT/possible pulmonary embolism, gangrene, diabetic foot infection/acute ischaemia, PAD, and poor-circulation warning signs.
